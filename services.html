Our Services

Creating unforgettable moments through design, planning, and passion across Lagos, Abuja, and Port Harcourt.

What We Offer

At Royal Dainties Decorations, we specialize in full-service event decoration and planning for all occasions. Whether you're popping the question, saying "I do," celebrating a birthday, or organizing a corporate gala — we've got you covered.

🎉 Birthday Decorations

Celebrate life's special milestones with our vibrant and stylish birthday decoration packages. Whether it's a chic adult party or a fun-themed celebration for kids, Royal Dainties Decorations creates the perfect ambiance with balloons, props, dessert stands, custom signage, and more. We handle the setup so you can focus on making memories.

Price Range: ₦250,000 – ₦1,500,000

💍 Proposal Setups

Make your "Will you marry me?" moment absolutely unforgettable. Our romantic proposal decoration packages include candle-lit setups, floral backdrops, custom signs, fairy lights, and location styling — from rooftops and gardens to beaches and private rooms. We help you plan the perfect surprise that your partner will talk about forever.

Price Range: ₦150,000 – ₦700,000

💒 Wedding Decorations

Your dream wedding deserves magical décor. From elegant stage setups to floral centerpieces, ceiling drapes, lighting, welcome signs, and couple's table styling, our wedding decoration services turn venues into breathtaking experiences. Whether intimate or grand, we tailor your big day to your vision — beautifully and professionally.

Price Range: ₦800,000 – ₦2,500,000

🥂 Bridal Shower

Treat the bride-to-be to a stunning bridal shower filled with charm and elegance. We offer personalized decor themes with balloon arches, floral walls, lounge areas, brunch setups, and pamper stations. Whether it's boho, glam, garden, or spa-inspired, we create a day she and her girls will cherish forever.

Price Range: ₦250,000 – ₦800,000

💼 Corporate Events

Impress clients and elevate your brand with stylish, professional corporate event decoration. From product launches and award nights to seminars and conferences, we design high-impact visual setups that reflect your brand's identity. Our team handles logistics, design, and decor so you can focus on your message.

Price Range: ₦1,500,000 – ₦5,000,000
